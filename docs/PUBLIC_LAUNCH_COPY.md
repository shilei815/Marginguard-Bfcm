# MarginGuard

**Plan Smarter Discounts. Protect Your Profit.**

See how your promotions affect profit, protect important products, and find
better discount plans.

MarginGuard helps small e-commerce merchants plan everyday, seasonal, clearance,
launch and storewide promotions. Upload a product CSV, enter offers, set your
minimum profit goal, and review a suggested plan that respects your hard rules.
Priority Tiers help protect important products while recovering profit from
more flexible offers.

Your product and financial inputs are processed locally in your browser. There
is no merchant-data upload API, account or database in this beta. Download reports
you need before refreshing; campaign state is held in memory.

Profit After Variable Costs means modeled contribution profit, not final
accounting net profit. Results use fixed expected sales volume and do not forecast
additional demand from discounts. Check missing costs, hard-rule warnings and
actual checkout discounts before launching a promotion. Continuous suggested
offers may become infeasible when rounded by a commerce platform.

Start with the [merchant guide](BETA_USER_GUIDE.md) and review the
[known limitations](BETA_KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md). This is beta planning software;
verify assumptions and checkout behavior independently before making commercial
decisions. No guarantee of future sales or profit is made.
